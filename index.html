What is Echomir?

Echomir is a mirrored alphabet cipher created by Xstream. Letters are paired across a phone keyboard’s centerline, giving familiar words an unfamiliar appearance.

It preserves the structure of your text. Every pairing works in both directions, so the same translator encodes and decodes.

A creative way to write and share messages—not encryption for private information.
